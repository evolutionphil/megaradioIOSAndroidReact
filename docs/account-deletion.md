# MegaRadio account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your MegaRadio account and its associated data in the app or by email, including when you no longer have the app installed.

## Delete your account in MegaRadio

1. Open MegaRadio and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2. Open **Profile** and choose **Delete Account**.
3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

## Request deletion without the app

Email **[privacy@themegaradio.com](mailto:privacy@themegaradio.com?subject=MegaRadio%20account%20deletion%20request)** with the subject **MegaRadio account deletion request**.

Include the email address or username associated with your MegaRadio account and state that you want to delete the account and its associated data. Send the request from the registered email address where possible. Do not send your password, verification codes, payment-card details or purchase tokens.

## Data covered by the request

Account deletion covers your MegaRadio account profile, favorites and listening history. MegaRadio's existing in-app privacy notice states that personal data is removed within 30 days after account deletion.

Google Play purchase and subscription records are managed separately by Google. Deleting a MegaRadio account does not replace managing or cancelling a store subscription. To avoid future renewals, review or cancel your subscription in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Apple subscribers should use their Apple subscription settings.

For questions about deleting your account or associated data, contact **privacy@themegaradio.com**. See the [MegaRadio privacy policy](https://themegaradio.com/en/pages/privacy-policy) for further information.
